# Day action authority and scarce investigation time

Status: approved direction; implementation proceeds continuously through the three phases below. User authorization already includes design and implementation; no additional design approval checkpoint is required. Baseline inspected: `51f6da6`, with `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agent-story-contract.md`, and the September 14 live gateway evaluation authoritative over older plans.

## Product contract

An input attempts an action. Program rules resolve what can happen, how much of it happens before an interruption, what it costs, and which facts it can expose. Writer dramatizes the highlights of that elapsed interval. One response is a narrative turn, never a completed loop.

- Short inquiry costs 20–30 minutes; normal inquiry/investigation costs 45–60 minutes; deep investigation costs 90–120 minutes. Default quotes are 25, 55, and 105 minutes respectively.
- Travel is additional, charged once per actual leg through `estimateTravel`; two actions at one destination share that leg. The 08:00–16:00 investigation window is 480 minutes, with a tuning target of 5–8 investigations; a full calendar day targets roughly 10–16 major actions. These are tuning targets, never hard turn caps.
- Opening an observation, clue, character, map, inventory, or already-generated information panel costs nothing. A generated inquiry prompted from that panel is a priced action.
- An explicit short budget limits scope. “Spend five minutes investigating everything” grants only the executed preliminary action, never the full investigation's findings at a discount. A two-hour allowance is a maximum, not mandatory filler. Multiple attempted stages aggregate costs in execution order.
- Scheduled events stop execution at their boundary. Charge only executed time and resources. Do not reveal the reward for unfinished work. Offer explicit remaining work after the event; resume never recharges completed travel or work. Midnight/reset invalidates that continuation.
- Days 1–3 cannot reveal solution/confirmation or commit normal route endings; `cycleCount >= 4` is necessary, never sufficient. Suspicion persists but increases at most +15 per actor per repeated day, tied to new authorized evidence.
- Writer never receives canonical truth and never owns state. State cannot own time, resources resolved by the action authority, loops, routes, endings, facts, memory ledgers, action continuations, or opportunity counters.

## Phase 1: factual and protocol boundaries

Introduce a projected authorization ledger containing only allowed text: authorized revelation projections, already-known projected facts, verified public opening events, authorized background history, accepted sourced event records, and later resolved action results. Each source has an ID, kind, text, optional fact ID/reveal level, and optional allowed speakers. A player's assertion, generated beat, summary, or arbitrary old assistant text is never an independent factual source. Previously accepted prose can support “NPC said X” as a disclosure event; it cannot make X canonical.

Replace the narrative critic's bare approval with a structured assertion audit. The critic examines maintext, options, summary, hint, observation and generated checklist descriptions; returns each material assertion's exact quote/span, proposition, source citations, status (`supported`, `unsupported`, `contradicted`, `question`, `hypothesis`, `ordinary-present`), and explanation. Semantic work is still model work: we do not claim deterministic natural-language entailment. The program verifies source IDs, cited substrings, source kind/delivery restrictions, exact narrative quote, complete audit shape, and that each unsupported/contradicted assertion blocks. Require an explicit coverage declaration for every reviewed narrative field; missing or malformed audit is a recoverable review failure, not approval. Existing high-specificity regressions remain deterministic sentinels, not a replacement for semantic coverage. No growing “dangerous words” list is presented as proof of grounding.

Questions, explicitly tentative hypotheses, ordinary present actions (“the clerk handed over water”), and authorized paraphrases remain playable. Negative claims are not automatically safe: “there was no login record,” “she never arrived,” and “the footage was deleted” are evidence and need their own sources. “I received no reply during this attempt” is an executed communication outcome, not a claim about login records, reading, deletion, or earlier whereabouts. The 06:50 message proves what she said, not attendance; its time must not attach to the untimed breakfast note. White delivery van/bread/milk/06:30 is rejected absent that entire supported proposition, even if an unrelated authorized fact contains a time, vehicle, purchase, or record.

Preserve whole-scene repair and re-review. Do not silently delete an offending line and leave its answer, summary, hint, option, or later memory in place. A confirmed fact remains allowed at its authorized level, but the presence of a confirmation never pardons another detail. On review/API/protocol failure there is no playback, resource charge, knowledge change, or memory commit.

Registered location validation is shared at plan hard review, State validation, compatibility sanitation, and transaction boundary. Do not register `police_station` solely to legitimize a generated mistake. An attempt to visit an unavailable place can stay at a registered street/current location and obtain an ordinary limitation or communication attempt. Do not reject all reasonable unsupported destinations as supernatural fantasy.

## Phase 2: immutable resolved actions

Add `engine/action-resolution.ts`, a pure authority module with exported types. Its inputs include the current state, stable logical action ID, validated intent steps, explicit player budget, registered destination, and the next blocking scheduled event. Director proposes intent and scope, not final costs/results. Fixed UI action/opportunity IDs are parsed directly; free input receives a validated structured intent proposal. Keep the existing intent fantasy/diversion policy. A deterministic fallback is a normal scoped inquiry with no unearned evidence; unresolved ambiguity must not mint a deep result cheaply.

The resolver outputs ordered segments, planned/executed minutes, start/end clock, before/after resources, executed arrival, completed outcome/source IDs, and an optional continuation. Travel uses existing map estimates; inquiry rates default to 3/7/14 stamina for short/normal/deep and zero routine sanity loss. Long physical searches use 6/14/24 stamina. Rest restores 12 stamina per 60 executed minutes, capped by the existing 120 maximum. Existing scripted costs (fantasy −8 sanity, death news −12 once) are separate event effects, deduplicated by effect ID; routine investigation is not automatically psychologically damaging. Tune rates only with measured full-day trajectories. Resource descriptions use resolved before/after bands; subjective distress is allowed but objective exhaustion cannot accompany full stamina.

Compute partial segment cost as a difference of cumulative cost, e.g. `round(totalCost * executedTotal / plannedTotal) - alreadyChargedCost`. This preserves conservation across multiple interruptions. Effects attached to a milestone occur only once that milestone is actually reached. A completed travel segment followed by partial work leaves the player at the destination; interrupted travel leaves location at the registered origin/transit representation until arrival is real. Preserve remaining travel time so resume does not restart its full cost.

The 16:00 event and midnight are explicit boundaries. If already at a pending boundary, deliver the event first; do not begin new investigation work and then claim its reward. For an action ending exactly at the boundary, completed work can be recorded if its completion precedes the event delivery, and the event is pending/delivered exactly once according to the existing event protocol. Longer quiet waits can advance to the next event/appointment or requested budget; remove the old 180-minute ceiling only for resolver-approved compressed waits. No generated turn needs a synthetic one-minute add-on when an already-due event is delivered at its boundary; local panels remain zero-cost and generated inquiry actions retain positive executed work.

Resolution precedes final WriterPacket construction. Rebuild/filter destination scene contract and revelation list against executed segments; otherwise the writer can visit an unexecuted destination or disclose an unfinished investigation's result. The immutable object is cached with the prepared turn and included in preplan keys by clock, cycle, location, resources, opportunities and continuation. Retries reuse it, stale preparations invalidate it. No state is changed until persistence and guarded commit. Stable resolution IDs plus the existing turn lifecycle guard prevent duplicate settlement; reroll restores the pre-turn snapshot including continuation, resource effects and memory.

WriterPacket receives only resolved public outcome projections, cost/clock/resource facts, and approved available knowledge. It is instructed to show the significant beats of 55 minutes, not narrate 55 minutes of dialogue. A dedicated action consistency check compares asserted elapsed duration, arrival, completion, rest/exhaustion and reward to this object. The assertion audit treats exact action duration/resource outcome as authorized sources; model comparison catches paraphrases while explicit numeric conflicts have deterministic tests.

UI quote comes from the same pricing function. ActionPanel, map travel and normal choices show approximate work + travel total and resulting boundary limitation when known; free-action input discloses the pricing bands and entered budget without requesting a confirmation dialog on every action. The next choices explicitly name remaining work and its remaining quote after an interruption.

## Phase 3: opportunities, compression and character cognition

Build opportunities from the existing fact gate's usable projections and authored action affordances. No new canonical route facts are necessary. For each relevant registered destination, run the fact gate with destination context, preserving cycle, route, evidence, suspicion and identity rules; offer a public investigative goal without leaking hidden fact IDs/answers. Current-location short/normal/deep work selects a reachable milestone; travel facts remain unavailable until arrival. Repeated work can upgrade an authorized reveal level or test a distinct hypothesis, but not award the same evidence or suspicion twice. At least one affordable, currently attainable opportunity is offered first when available; if none exists, explain the limitation and offer a meaningful next location, rest, or an explicit compressed wait. Do not force 2–4 fabricated clues to fill a list.

Progress is a new authorized fact/level, new independently supported character knowledge, a resolved hypothesis, a completed meaningful action, or an actual scheduled event. A changed weather sentence, repeated refusal or restatement does not reset the stagnation counter. After two consecutive no-progress actions with the same target/topic, mark that opportunity exhausted for this day/condition and prioritize alternatives; the player can still insist, receiving an honest bounded attempt and optional wait, never an invisible cancellation. Default repeated waits compress through the quiet interval to the next actionable event, appointment or requested limit. Compression does not skip an available time-limited opportunity without disclosing it, or skip death/reset transitions.

Extend the existing world-memory model with four separate concepts: what an observer knows, believes/suspects, has actually said to a listener, and has committed to do. All refer to source events/reviewed spans; explicit player assertions can become `heard` or `believed`, never canonical truth. The same proposition can be known by the player and unknown to an NPC. An NPC's previous lie is a disclosure record, not evidence of truth; its public stance need not expose hidden canonical knowledge to Writer. Commitments require an actual accepted promise/appointment (speaker, recipient, action, location, due clock, cycle), not a player request. The ledger records active, fulfilled, cancelled, expired/reset status with cause.

On reset retain player facts, player cognition, durable suspicion, and historical events/episodes. Reset NPC active day-acquired cognition to authored pre-day baseline; retain their historical disclosure records only as player recollection, not present-day NPC memory. Reset name introductions for NPCs who did not know the player at baseline. Mark all day commitments and action continuations expired/reset; replay cannot fulfill them in the new cycle. Do not delete historical episodes that the player remembers. Save migration must not infer that every legacy NPC cognition is baseline: only known authored baseline markers qualify; day-scoped unknown-source NPC learning is excluded from active cognition after reset. Existing v2 world memory is normalized without data loss into the new optional fields.

Full-day action counts are reported against tuning targets, not asserted as guaranteed for every player strategy. Gate regressions must pass. Real model samples report facts, contradictions, failed attempts, effective turns, major actions, exact game minutes, resources, interruptions, request counts and playable latency. A test process exiting zero means collection finished, not game-quality acceptance. Provider failure is recorded separately; do not claim unrun scenarios passed or omit them. No keys or raw credentials in committed reports.

## Scope limits

Keep the existing React/Zustand/Vitest architecture, truth graph, role pipeline, ending gates and persistence guard. Add focused authority modules and small optional structured fields rather than a scheduler rewrite or replacement narrative engine. The three phases are sequential because opportunity rewards and cognition must consume resolved, authorized outcomes.
